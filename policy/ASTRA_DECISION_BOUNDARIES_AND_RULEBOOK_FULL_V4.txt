ASTRA DECISION BOUNDARIES AND RULEBOOK

1. Objective, sector definition and unit of analysis (DOCDB family)

Team 12 | Politecnico di Torino | Innovation Management | Enel electricity-storage landscape | V4 |
19 November 2026

The question for each family is whether its principal technical contribution concerns stationary
industrial or utility/network-serving storage for subsequent delivery as electricity. The unit is
one original DOCDB family. Several members, kinds or claims may inform that decision, but each
family receives one vote and one label. A specific, technically developed embodiment can establish
the family’s relevance, including when other embodiments serve different markets. Being useful to
an electricity company, being patented by an energy specialist, or appearing in a storage search
does not establish that relationship.

Start with three notes in ordinary language. Identify what the invention changes, the path from
charging or energy capture through retention to electrical delivery, and the installation or
service that uses it. For a support invention, identify where it acts on that path. Apply the two
gates below before choosing a segment or counting families.

2. What counts as electricity storage and what fails Gate S

Gate S asks whether storing energy for subsequent electrical output, or technically enabling that
function, is material to the disclosed contribution. Find a retained quantity, a time separation
and an electrical release function. Common cases are charge in a battery, liquid head, pressurized
air, angular momentum, working electrolyte, heat subsequently used by a generator, or an
electrically produced fuel reserved for later generation. Power electronics alone convert or route
energy. A tank alone establishes only that something is kept. Identify what it keeps and why.

A part need not claim the complete chain. A reservoir that changes usable discharge capacity in a
purpose-built CAES plant acts directly on storage. A network controller that commands charging of
an identified electricity-storage system acts on the charge leg even if the abstract omits its
inverter. Both require evidence of what system the part serves. A generic compressor, a DC link
that merely smooths pulses, or a transmission path that can pass regenerative energy straight to
another load does not establish a store-and-release contribution.

For enabling inventions, look for a storage-specific decision, measurement or intervention. State
of charge constraining grid response, a discharge-rate setpoint, equipment sizing derived from
cycling limits and a cleaning mode for a flow-battery stack are examples. For simulation or
planning, a technical resource parameter, capacity, location, feasible response or schedule must
depend on storage. A financial score, abstract energy trade or generic reserve assignment in a
system that happens to contain a battery does not suffice.

3. What establishes utility, network or industrial relevance (Gate U)

Identify the electrical beneficiary and the service. Examples include a store at a generating
station smoothing its export, an aggregate fleet responding to distribution demand, a microgrid
balancing several sources and loads, or retained energy returned across the buses of an industrial
plant. An islanded network can qualify. A grid connection available for charging, a household
selling surplus, a mobile device connected to mains or a utility tariff on a bill establishes
little about the function of the store.

A second route is objective plant architecture. Examples include a chamber and expander train, a
pumped reservoir discharging through a generator, a tank-and-stack flow system, or an
electricity-storage retrofit integrated with existing generating and transmission equipment. Look
for the arrangement of equipment, its duty and its connections. Do not infer plant scale from a
word such as modular, microgrid, industrial or high-power in isolation. Likewise, a device
installed at a factory can still be a process load with no in-scope storage function.

There is no fixed MW or MWh threshold. A common battery controlling demand across several dwelling
units provides a network service even without a rating. A high-power traction battery can fail the
same test. Record EXPLICIT_NETWORK_OR_INDUSTRIAL for an evidenced context or service and
PROVEN_PLANT_ARCHITECTURE when the engineered installation establishes applicability. In an
excluded row, a network context may be explicit while Gate S fails. NOT_ESTABLISHED records an
unproved link. EXPLICITLY_OUT_OF_SCOPE records an identified end use outside the sector.

4. Precise label definitions and decision tree

CORE. Both gates pass and the invention materially changes the physical ability to capture, retain
or release energy, the working medium, or the integrated physical charge/store/discharge
arrangement. A subsystem can satisfy this definition. Adding conventional batteries to an equipment
list does not by itself change the storage mechanism.

ENABLING. Both gates pass and the principal contribution operates, sizes, interconnects, protects,
diagnoses or maintains the qualifying store. A converter, control cabinet or maintenance circuit
can be tangible equipment and still perform an enabling function. Under the Team 12 rule of 17
November, dispatch or peak-clipping/valley-filling of an existing BESS is ENABLING.

IRRELEVANT. The supplied evidence fails at least one gate, or the invention changes another
function and its reference to storage is incidental. The reason should name the missing
relationship or actual end use. An excluded family can still disclose a real battery or operate on
a real electricity network.

Decision tree
  1. Identify the principal contribution in the strongest available technical source.
  2. Does it implement storage for electrical delivery or specifically enable such a store?
     No or unestablished -> IRRELEVANT. State what it actually does or what is missing.
  3. Does the store serve a qualified network or industrial installation, or does its architecture establish plant use?
     No or unestablished -> IRRELEVANT. State the end use or missing deployment link.
  4. Does the main advance change the physical storage medium, mechanism or integrated storage plant?
     Yes -> CORE. Otherwise, if it technically enables the qualified store -> ENABLING.
  5. Set one segment and the decisive rule. Verify the excerpt against the identified source.

5. Operational rulebook R01–R12

R01 | Storage absent. Use when the disclosed service can be traced entirely through generation,
transmission, load or metering, with no material electrical store. Peak following by a turbine
governor is excluded. Peak following by issuing battery discharge setpoints qualifies under R04.

R02 | Storage present, qualifying setting missing. Exclude a working device or interface
demonstrated only in a dedicated appliance, a transport platform or an unspecified installation. An
isolated base-station battery or a converter with unspecified external loads fails. An offshore
industrial network sharing recovered electricity across multiple load buses can pass.

R03 | Qualified physical mechanism. Include as CORE when the medium, reservoir, active component or
integrated plant arrangement materially changes electrical storage or recovery. Identify the
physical connection and the qualifying installation. An expander coupled to a generator can
qualify. Modeling the expander’s operating schedule is a different contribution.

R04 | Storage-specific technical support. Include as ENABLING when a qualified store is the object
of dispatch, sizing, control, protection, maintenance or technically necessary integration.
Physical construction of a power interface may qualify under this rule. A network schedule that
could make identical decisions for generic generators, or a ranking of financial benefits with no
technical output, fails.

R05 | Heat, hydrogen and other carriers. Trace the output beyond the heat tank, electrolyser or
fuel store. Heat sold as heat, hydrogen produced for use as a commodity, or a fuel cell consuming
externally supplied fuel fails the electricity-storage test. Electrical input converted to fuel,
retained and later used for electrical generation can pass. Decide separately whether the disclosed
advance is the cycle or its control. A secondary heat or commodity output does not invalidate a
demonstrated electrical branch.

R06 | EVs and V2G. Require a storage-specific network service, such as requesting discharge from
vehicle batteries for an aggregate distribution peak. Managing traction or delaying drivers’
charging represents vehicle operation or flexible demand alone. A regulation-market price used by a
transport asset does not demonstrate a technical grid response.

R07 | Household and microgrid. Single-premises self-consumption, backup or occasional export is
excluded in the absence of another qualifying function. Common storage acting on a multi-user
network, storage responding to external operator instructions, and storage balancing an industrial
or isolated power network can pass. Check which load is being balanced and what triggers discharge.

R08 | Charging stations. Trace the stationary store and identify any service beyond supplying
vehicle chargers. A local buffer for the charging gun fails by itself. Feeder-constrained storage
sizing, coordinated control of grid import and storage output for an established grid peak, or
actual discharge to the local grid during shortage can qualify. Station hardware does not
automatically imply CORE.

R09 | Conventional hydro. Require an identifiable storage-charging process, such as pumping water
to a head or expelling water from a submerged reservoir, and later generation from the stored
hydraulic potential. Turbine regulation, conventional reservoir hydropower and changing demand to
regulate water level fail without that link. Qualified pumped-storage plant hardware is classified
under R03 and storage-specific operation under R04.

R10 | Battery materials and parts. Determine whether the part actively participates in storage or
merely monitors or maintains it, then establish Gate U. An installation-specific
working-electrolyte arrangement can be CORE. Pack balancing or fault detection in a qualifying
plant is ENABLING. A new module connector, cell string or BMS with no demonstrated sector
application is excluded.

R11 | Insufficient technical or sector evidence. Use when evidence leaves a decisive link open, for
example whether a compensated voltage or peak serves an electrical network, or whether a named
energy store delivers electricity. Specify that link. This rule is not triggered by lack of EP text
alone.

R12 | Principal contribution elsewhere. Separate the improvement from its equipment list. Generic
reserve allocation, factory test sequencing, economic scoring or PV interconnection can all mention
batteries without operating storage for the qualifying service. Conversely, a storage-specific
optimizer remains ENABLING even when its apparatus description lists a complete plant.

One primary rule records the decisive branch. R03 was primary for qualifying physical inventions.
R04 was the usual primary for support, with R06, R07 or R08 primary where the transport,
residential or charging boundary drove the positive decision. For exclusions, a specific end-use
rule takes precedence when it directly explains the result. R01 indicates a disclosed non-storage
operation, R12 an identifiable contribution elsewhere, and R11 a missing link in an otherwise
plausible storage disclosure. Optional secondary codes identify additional tests. They do not
create additional labels.

6. Borderline doctrines and technology segments

Grid EMS and converters. Name the manipulated quantity and its target. Charge limits used to
calculate deliverable regulation, or a bidirectional interface designed to connect station
batteries, qualify. Generic inverter synchronization or a schedule of generic participant reserves
does not. Peak is an operating condition, not a technology. Determine whether it changes
generation, customer demand or stored-energy dispatch.

Battery and flow components. A working-electrolyte tank that participates in rebalancing the active
inventory changes storage capacity. An added acid-cleaning circuit preserves the operation of an
existing stack. The distinction turns on what the added circuit changes. Ordinary cells, pack
electronics and converters need positive deployment evidence even when their battery function is
explicit.

Homes, vehicles, charging and microgrids. Ask who calls for service and where the power goes. A
home selling surplus at its own discretion, a vehicle charging more slowly and a charging site
smoothing its internal voltage are each insufficient alone. An operator instruction, a defined
community peak, an upstream constraint used in storage sizing, or grid injection at times of
shortage provides an observable distinction. A stationary storage plant may use mobile containers
to transfer energy between named generating and receiving stations. Mobility is assessed in the
context of the principal service.

Thermal and hydrogen routes. Identify retention and the pathway back to electricity. Thermal bodies
driving a generator qualify even where the abstract omits the original source of the heat. Thermal
demand shifting for space heat fails. An electrolyser may provide frequency response by changing
demand without storing electricity for subsequent delivery. Where a battery clearly supplies a
power network but the hydrogen branch is open, assess the battery contribution on its own evidence.

CAES, flywheels and hydro. Distinguish a purpose-built charge/release reservoir from a compressor
feeding an unspecified use. Air used only to cool a separate gas generator does not establish
electrical recovery of the stored air. Flywheel arrays providing network frequency response
qualify, whereas a rotor buffer serving only turbine pitch/yaw drives fails the network test.
Hydroelectric generation needs a demonstrated charge stage. For a hybrid plant choose the principal
storage arrangement, retaining coexisting media in the narrative.

Segment is assigned after the label. BESS covers identified battery systems. FLOW_BATTERY requires
evidence of flow-battery technology. PUMPED_HYDRO includes equivalent engineered hydraulic
charge/recovery. COMPRESSED_AIR_LIQUID_AIR groups pressurized or liquefied air storage.
GRAVITY_FLYWHEEL_MECHANICAL covers relevant mass or rotor storage. THERMAL_TO_ELECTRICITY and
POWER_TO_X_TO_POWER require their respective return pathways. CROSS_CUTTING_STORAGE is used for
operation spanning stores, or storage-specific methods where the medium is generic. OTHER_IN_SCOPE
covers qualified architectures outside a single listed type, including some hybrid or incompletely
specified physical designs. Exclusions use OUT_OF_SCOPE regardless of the technologies they
mention.

7. Source ranking, publication versions and language

First establish the principal contribution in the independent claims of a verified publication when
supplied. Consider dependent claims for a concrete storage embodiment. Read the description of that
same publication to interpret equipment, energy paths and beneficiaries. Distinguish a specifically
developed storage embodiment from an optional battery in a list. Use the original title and
abstract where relevant, and as the principal technical evidence in the absence of matched full
text. CPC/IPC, applicants and bibliographic links may corroborate context but cannot establish a
missing gate. An abstract quote is evidence of disclosure and is not presented as claim language.

For a multi-publication family, compare the actual A and B texts. A particular member can establish
a qualifying embodiment. Record the full exact publication number and its kind for the passage
used. Do not combine an independent claim from one member with a description from another and
attribute the combination to a single patent. The policy concerns evidence anywhere in the supplied
family and does not determine present enforceable rights by jurisdiction. Seven families use a
quoted publication other than the CSV-selected member.

For family 59685989 the granted EP3422521B1 expressly ties switchable converters to batteries and a
power system. For 43450072 the EP2449259B1 generator/expansion provisions support the physical
storage decision. For 34931966 the application EP3266997A1 specifically includes later
generating-plant use of the produced fuel. The other formulations in this family were not read as
having identical claims. Where a broader independent claim is generic, the description or a
narrower claim can establish a specific embodiment without changing the wording of the broad claim.

Use the individual language fields. Among the 55 publications cited, 48 have English claims and
English descriptions, four have English claims and German descriptions, and three have both in
German. CSV excerpts reproduce their source verbatim, including German where used. The English
rationales explain their significance. They are analyst summaries, not quoted English translations.
The excerpt identifies one decisive passage. For a negative decision, the omitted link is
established by considering the available record, not by treating silence in that short quotation as
proof.

8. Exclusion when sector evidence is insufficient

An excluded family can have a real storage mechanism with an unidentified deployment, or a proven
network service that does not require storage. Record those dimensions separately.
EXPLICIT_PHYSICAL and EXPLICIT_STORAGE_SUPPORT may therefore occur on excluded rows.
NO_ELECTRIC_STORAGE refers to the disclosed function. NOT_ESTABLISHED indicates a missing or merely
incidental relationship. For a mixed-use case, identify any documented qualifying branch before
excluding it.

State the narrow evidentiary limit, such as an unidentified beneficiary of peak shifting, absent
electrical recovery from heat, or lack of a storage-specific control quantity. Apply the same
standard across jurisdictions, document availability and original reviewer partitions. Missing
European publication text describes retrieval coverage. It is neither evidence against a family nor
a reason to include it. A sufficiently informative original abstract can support CORE or ENABLING.
A supplied grant can still support IRRELEVANT. A scope exclusion makes no prediction about
physically possible adaptations or material that a future search might retrieve.

9. Reproducibility, provenance and limitations

The supplied Team12_ASTRA_V4_RECLASSIFICATION_KIT.zip contains ASTRA_PROMPT_RECLASSIFICATION_V4.md,
Team12_300_ENRICHED_FOR_ASTRA.csv, Team12_FAMILY_EVIDENCE_FULL.jsonl and manifest.json. The
manifest reports FULL extraction in PATSTAT PROD and EPAB PROD at 2026-11-19T20:43:34.918490Z.
Family CPC was extracted from TLS225_DOCDB_FAM_CPC. No separate database edition number was
supplied in the manifest. No external patent lookup was used. The input files were checked against
the kit’s supplied SHA-256 values. Annotation was performed on 19 November 2026 by Codex (GPT-6).

Pass one applied the V4 two-gate boundary to each family in its original order, with independent
judgments recorded in four batches of 75. Existing labels and reviewer flags were not used to set
outcomes. All 300 JSONL records were decoded. For the 55 families with full text, the selected
claims and description were matched to the same publication in JSONL. The latter also supplied 33
further full-text records, giving 88 publication records with claims and descriptions. For each,
the supplied claims were segmented to locate independent provisions and relevant dependent
provisions. Description text was scanned in overlapping windows for the problem, solution,
storage/recovery path and sector use. Targeted passages were assessed with their publication
identity intact.

The longest selected description was 813,789 characters. It was processed in sections. EPAB text
sometimes flattens claim numbering and punctuation. Claim-boundary identification in those cases is
interpretive. The procedure covered the full input files but did not entail a line-by-line human
reading of every description or a legal analysis of all claims. The evidence_limitation field
identifies targeted description use, missing source material and material gaps for individual
decisions.

Pass two compared families grouped by principal contribution, rule and final outcome. Comparisons
included technical sizing versus economic scoring, peak dispatch versus physical storage
construction, shared versus individual domestic storage, grid delivery versus charger demand,
demand-side hydrogen versus fuel-to-power cycles, storage chemistry versus maintenance, and
conventional versus charged hydro. Decisions were revised where a stated frequency, local voltage,
market or title did not substantiate the inferred service. No quotas or count targets were applied.

12. Appropriate interpretation

These are 300 final AI-assisted policy classifications of the supplied family evidence. They answer
the defined electricity-storage landscape question. The annotation status for every family is
AI_POLICY_CLASSIFICATION_NOT_HUMAN_VALIDATED. In this dataset, counting CORE alone answers a
narrower question about physical storage inventions. Counting CORE plus ENABLING also includes
technical support of qualified systems. Keep that distinction when reporting totals.

The results do not establish legal novelty or granted scope for every family, describe every
embodiment in every jurisdiction, or provide human-validated ground truth. The uneven availability
of EP evidence affects what can be demonstrated for particular families. Precision and recall
require an independent reference standard and an explicitly defined evaluation population. Neither
metric is estimated by this exercise.
